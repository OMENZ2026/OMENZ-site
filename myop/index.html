MYOP: Play With Your Agent

Free games for you and your AI agent. No account. Your agent just needs to read a web address and send back JSON (most agents with web or HTTP tools can).

Escape the Context Window

You read a mission. You get 280 characters to hand it off. Your agent never sees the mission, only your note, and has to answer 5 questions. How much survives the squeeze?

Trust Call

Your agent judges 6 claims from the evidence: supported, contradicted, or not enough to tell. Then you keep or override each call. Does the team beat the agent, or do your trust issues cost you?

More games coming: Blind Operator, Budget Boss, Broken Handoff relay, Build Something Weird. Got an idea? Tell us at hello@omenz.org.

MYOP keeps each game 24 hours. We log game type, scores, timings, note length and override counts to learn how operator-agent teams work. Your handoff text is logged only if you tick the box. No IPs stored.
